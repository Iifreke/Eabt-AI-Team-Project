Edutech Admissions Platform: Complete Staff SOP & System Flows

Operational Handbook for Admissions Officers, Support Teams, and Technical Administrators managing Babcock University & Ahmadu Bello University distance learning admissions.

📖 Part 1: How the System Works (In Plain English)

The Edutech Admissions Platform acts as an intelligent bridge connecting prospective students with university admissions officers. It guarantees that inquiries received through either Web Chat or WhatsApp are answered immediately, qualified accurately, and synced to Zoho CRM.

🤖 1. AI Concierge (24/7)

Answers tuition, course requirements, and deadlines using official university brochures. Never invents details or hallucinates.

🧑‍💼 2. Live Human Advisor

Available Mon–Fri (8 AM – 6 PM WAT). Students can request an advisor, triggering instant alerts to Zoho Cliq and staff dashboard.

🎫 3. Offline Support Tickets

Outside office hours, students raise tickets. Staff replies on the dashboard are automatically emailed directly to the student.

📋 Part 2: Step-by-Step Daily Staff Workflow

Step 1: Logging in & Setting Online Presence

  1. Visit https://eabt-ai-team-project.vercel.app
  2. Enter your staff credentials (e.g. eabtconnect@gmail.com).
  3. Confirm your presence indicator in the bottom-left sidebar turns 🟢 Online.

Step 2: Attending Live Chats & Escalations

1
Instant Alert Fired

Student requests human advisor → Alert card posts to Zoho Cliq & badge turns red on Dashboard.

2
Direct Access

Click "Open Live Chat in Admin Portal" in Zoho Cliq to jump directly into the conversation.

3
Take Over Chat

Click "Attend / Take Over". The AI pauses and informs the student an advisor is now online.

4
Reply (Web & WhatsApp)

Type message or use quick reply shortcuts (/takeover, /review, /close).

5
Resolve & Sync

Add staff notes and click "Mark as Resolved". Full transcript syncs to Zoho CRM automatically.

⚡ Quick Reply Shortcuts Cheat Sheet

Shortcut Purpose Default Response Text
/takeover Joining live chat "Hi, this conversation has been escalated to an admissions advisor. I am reviewing your inquiry now..."
/review Checking requirements "Thank you for your patience. I am currently reviewing your academic requirements and application details."
/wait Consulting admissions registry "Thank you for your patience while I confirm this detail with the admissions office."
/portal Application portal assistance "If you are experiencing issues with the admissions portal, please share your application number..."
/close Concluding conversation "Thank you for contacting our Admissions Directorate. We wish you great success in your academic pursuit!"

🎫 Part 3: Offline Ticket Management & Direct Email Replies

Outside working hours, students can submit an inquiry ticket with their full name, verified email, and question.

1. Student Submits Ticket

The student fills the offline ticket modal on the web widget. A confirmation email is instantly dispatched to their inbox, and the ticket appears under Tickets on the dashboard.

2. Officer Replies from Dashboard

Staff clicks on the ticket, reviews the student's inquiry, types an official response, and clicks ✉️ Send Email Reply. An executive branded email is immediately sent to the student.

Student Ingestion Layer

✅ Part 5: Quality Checklist & Support

Admissions Best Practices

  • Never share personal banking info — direct to official portals.
  • Reply to all offline tickets within 24 hours.
  • Set your presence status to Offline when leaving your shift.
