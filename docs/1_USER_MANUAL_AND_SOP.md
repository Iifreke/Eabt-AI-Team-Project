## Edutech Babcock & ABU Admissions Intelligence Platform

## 🎯 Welcome & Overview

Welcome to the **Edutech Admissions & Live Support Platform**! This system is designed to help our university admissions team manage student inquiries effortlessly across both **Web Chat** and **WhatsApp**, while ensuring no prospective student is ever lost or ignored.

### 🌟 How the Platform Works in Simple Terms
1. **Instant AI Concierge (24/7)**: When a student visits the website or sends a WhatsApp message, our AI Assistant (**Maverick**) instantly greets them by name, answers questions about courses, fees, entry requirements, and application procedures using official university documents.
2. **Live Human Advisor Handoff (Working Hours)**: During office hours (Monday – Friday, 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM WAT), students can ask to speak with a human advisor. Staff members are alerted instantly on **Zoho Cliq** and on the **Admin Dashboard**, allowing an officer to take over the conversation in real-time.
3. **Offline Support Tickets (Outside Working Hours)**: Outside office hours, students can open a support ticket. All tickets appear in the dashboard, and when staff type a reply, it is **automatically emailed** to the student's registered email address.
4. **Automatic Zoho CRM Sync**: Every student who initiates a chat or submits a form is automatically saved as a **Lead / Contact** in Zoho CRM with their contact details, lead score (HOT / WARM / COLD), and full chat transcript.

## 💬 3. Daily Live Chat Workflow (Step-by-Step)

### Step 1: Receiving an Escalation Alert
When a student needs human help (e.g. they say *"Can I speak to an advisor?"* or have a complex inquiry):
- A sound and live notification banner will appear on the **Chats** page.
- An instant notification card is sent to your **Zoho Cliq Admissions Channel** with a clickable button: **`[Open Live Chat in Admin Portal]`**.
- Clicking the link takes you **directly to that student's live conversation**.

### Step 2: Taking Over the Live Chat
1. In the **Chats** table, click on the student's row (e.g. `👤 Amina Bello` or `📱 Emeka Obi (WhatsApp)`).
2. You will see the entire conversation transcript between the student and the AI.
3. Click the **`🚀 Attend / Take Over`** button.
4. The AI will gracefully pause, and a notification will tell the student: *"An admissions officer has joined the chat."*

### Step 3: Replying to the Student
- Type your message in the bottom reply box and press **Enter** or click **`Send Reply`**.
- If the student is on **WhatsApp**, your reply is delivered straight to their WhatsApp phone!
- If the student is on the **Web Chat**, your reply appears in their chat widget in real time.

### Step 4: Using Quick Reply Shortcuts (⚡ Speed Up Responses)
Instead of typing the same explanations repeatedly, use quick shortcuts:
- Click any of the **Quick Reply** buttons above the text box (or type the shortcut keyword):
  - **`/takeover`** → *"Hi, this conversation has been escalated to an admissions advisor. I am reviewing your inquiry now..."*
  - **`/review`** → *"Thank you for your patience. I am currently reviewing your academic requirements..."*
  - **`/wait`** → *"Thank you for your patience while I confirm this detail with the admissions office."*
  - **`/portal`** → *"If you are experiencing issues with the admissions portal, please share your application number..."*
  - **`/close`** → *"Thank you for contacting our Admissions Directorate. We wish you great success!"*

### Step 5: Resolving & Ending the Chat
When the student's questions have been fully answered:
1. Add any internal **Staff Notes** (e.g., *"Interested in MSc Economics, promised to apply by Friday"*).
2. Click **`✅ Mark as Resolved`**.
3. The conversation is archived, and the full transcript is synced to the student's profile in Zoho CRM.

## 🎫 4. Managing Offline Support Tickets (Step-by-Step)

When students visit outside working hours (after 6:00 PM WAT or on weekends), the chat widget allows them to submit an **Official Support Ticket**.

### How to process a ticket:
1. Click **Tickets** in the left menu.
2. You will see a list of tickets with status **Open** (Red badge), **In Progress** (Yellow badge), or **Closed** (Green badge).
3. Click on any ticket to expand it. You will see:
   - **Student Name & Validated Email Address**
   - **Inquiry Subject & Full Message**
4. In the **Official Email Reply** box, type your professional response to the student.
5. Click **`✉️ Send Email Reply`**.
6. **What happens instantly**:
   - The platform sends an official, luxury university-branded email directly to the student's inbox.
   - The ticket status automatically changes to **In Progress / Closed**.
   - A green confirmation badge displays: `✓ Reply emailed to student@email.com`.

## 📚 5. Updating University Knowledge Base

When admission fees, application deadlines, or academic requirements change:
1. Click **Knowledge Base** in the sidebar.
2. Select the relevant school (**Babcock** or **ABU**).
3. Drag and drop the official PDF document (e.g. `2026_Babcock_CODEL_Brochure.pdf` or `ABU_Tuition_Fees.pdf`).
4. Click **`Upload & Vectorize`**.
5. The system automatically reads the document and trains the AI concierge in under 10 seconds. From that moment on, the AI will use the new updated details to answer questions accurately!

## 🛡️ 6. Dos and Don'ts for Admissions Staff

### ✅ DOs:
- **Always address students by their name** (e.g., *"Dear Amina"*, *"Hello Mr. Bello"*).
- **Check Zoho Cliq alerts promptly** when on duty.
- **Add brief Staff Notes** before resolving chats so team members have context if the student calls back.
- **Log out or switch status to Offline** when leaving your desk.

### ❌ DON'Ts:
- **Do not share personal financial account numbers** — always refer students to the official university portal for tuition payments.
- **Do not leave tickets in Open status for more than 24 hours**.
- **Do not invent entry requirements or waiver policies** not stated in the official Knowledge Base.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (Staff FAQ)

**Q: What happens if I reply to a WhatsApp student while they are offline?**
> **A:** Your message is delivered to their WhatsApp phone as a standard text. They will receive it as soon as their phone is connected to the internet.

**Q: Can I see if the student is currently typing or active on the website?**
> **A:** Yes! The chat window displays real-time online status indicators.

**Q: Where can I see all student leads in Zoho CRM?**
> **A:** Log in to your Zoho CRM workspace. Click **Leads** or **Contacts**. Every student who fills the chat form or texts on WhatsApp appears there with lead tags and conversation history notes.
