1. System Overview

The Edutech School AI Chatbot is an AI-powered admissions assistant embedded on school websites. It allows prospective students from Babcock University and ABU (Ahmadu Bello University) to ask questions 24/7, get instant answers from a curated knowledge base, and connect with a live human support agent when needed.

The admin dashboard gives authorised staff full visibility into student leads, conversation histories, escalated support requests, and the ability to respond live to visitors in real time.

2. Access & Login

2.1 Logging In

  1. Navigate to https://eabt-ai-team-project.vercel.app
  2. Enter your official email address and password
  3. Click Sign in
  4. You will be redirected to the Dashboard
Note: If you were invited by a Super Admin, you must first click the link in your invitation email and set a password before you can log in normally.

2.2 First-Time Login (Invited Users)

  1. Check your email inbox for a message with subject: "You have been invited"
  2. Click Accept the invite
  3. You will be taken to the Set Password page
  4. Enter a password (minimum 8 characters) and confirm it
  5. Click Set Password & Continue
  6. You are now logged in and will be taken to the Dashboard
Important: Invite links expire after 24 hours. If your link has expired, ask a Super Admin to remove and re-invite you.

2.3 Signing Out

Click Sign out at the bottom of the left sidebar. Always sign out on shared computers.

4. Managing Team Members

This section applies to Super Admins only.

4.1 Inviting a New Team Member

1 Click Team Members in the left sidebar

2 Click the + Invite Member button (top right)

3 Fill in the form: Full Name, Email Address, and Role

4 Click Send Invite

5 The invitee will receive an email. They click the link and set their password.

Tip: Use the Admin role for most staff. Only give Super Admin to team leaders or system administrators.

4.2 Changing a Team Member's Role

  1. Go to Team Members
  2. Find the team member in the table
  3. In the Actions column, use the dropdown to select the new role
  4. The change takes effect immediately — no confirmation required

4.3 Removing a Team Member

  1. Go to Team Members
  2. Find the team member and click Remove
  3. Confirm the action in the dialog
  4. The user is immediately removed and loses all access
Caution: Removal is immediate and permanent. The removed user cannot log in again. To restore access, you must send a new invite.

6. Managing Leads

A Lead is a prospective student who has submitted their name, email, and phone number through the chat widget.

6.1 Viewing Leads

  1. Click Leads in the sidebar
  2. The table shows: Name, Email, Phone, School, and Date Added
  3. Use the school filter to narrow results

6.2 What to Do With a Lead

  • Follow up by email or phone for admissions enquiries
  • Check the lead's conversation history via the Conversations page
  • If the lead escalated a chat, handle it in the Escalations section

8. Handling Escalations (Live Chat)

When a visitor asks to speak to a human (e.g., "I want to talk to someone"), the bot escalates the conversation and notifies the visitor that a support agent will respond. The conversation moves to the Escalations queue.

8.1 Responding to an Escalation

1 Click Escalations in the sidebar

2 Open escalations are shown at the top. Click the row to open the live chat panel

3 Read the conversation history to understand the visitor's issue

4 Type your reply in the text box at the bottom and click Send

5 The visitor receives your message in real time (within 3–4 seconds)

6 Your name (as stored in your profile) will appear next to each reply

7 When the issue is resolved, click Mark Resolved

Note: The visitor can see who is responding — your full name is shown alongside each admin message. Ensure your profile name is set correctly.

8.2 Best Practice for Escalation Responses

  • Respond within 10 minutes during office hours
  • Greet the visitor by name if available
  • Keep messages clear and professional
  • If you need more time, send an acknowledgement: "Thanks for reaching out. Let me check this for you."
  • Always mark the conversation as Resolved when done — this clears it from the open queue

8.3 Out-of-Hours Escalations

If a visitor escalates outside office hours, the bot informs them that the support team will respond shortly. Ensure the first available admin reviews the Escalations queue at the start of each working day.

9. Managing the Knowledge Base

The Knowledge Base is the source of truth for the AI chatbot. It contains documents (PDFs, text files) that the bot searches when answering questions. Keeping it accurate and up to date is critical to the quality of bot responses.

9.1 Uploading a Document

1 Click Knowledge Base in the sidebar

2 Select the school this document belongs to (Babcock or ABU)

3 Click Upload Document or paste document text

4 Wait for processing — the system extracts and indexes the content

5 Once processed, the bot will use this document to answer questions

9.2 Deleting a Document

  1. Find the document in the Knowledge Base list
  2. Click Delete next to it
  3. Confirm the deletion
Warning: Deleting a document removes it from the AI's knowledge immediately. Only delete outdated or incorrect content.

9.3 What to Upload

  • Admissions brochures and prospectuses
  • Programme outlines and course descriptions
  • Fee schedules and scholarship information
  • Campus life guides
  • Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) documents
  • Contact directories

9.4 Keeping the Knowledge Base Current

Review and update the knowledge base at the start of each academic year and whenever:

  • Fee structures change
  • New programmes are introduced or discontinued
  • Admission requirements are updated
  • Campus facilities or services change

10. Embedding the Widget

The chat widget can be embedded on any school website by adding two script tags to the page's HTML. The widget loads as a floating chat bubble at the bottom-right of the screen.

10.1 Embed Code — Both Schools (Recommended)

Use this on a shared admissions or prospective students page that serves both universities:

<script>
  window.SchoolBotConfig = {
    schools: [
      { id: 'backock', name: 'Babcock University' },
      { id: 'abu',     name: 'ABU (Ahmadu Bello University)' }
    ],
    apiUrl: 'https://eabt-ai-team-project.vercel.app',
    theme: { primaryColor: '#1a73e8' }
  };
</script>
<script src="https://eabt-ai-team-project.vercel.app/widget.js"></script>

10.2 Embed Code — Single School

Use this when embedding on a school-specific website:

<!-- Babcock University only -->
<script>
  window.SchoolBotConfig = {
    schoolId: 'backock',
    apiUrl: 'https://eabt-ai-team-project.vercel.app'
  };
</script>
<script src="https://eabt-ai-team-project.vercel.app/widget.js"></script>

<!-- ABU only -->
<script>
  window.SchoolBotConfig = {
    schoolId: 'abu',
    apiUrl: 'https://eabt-ai-team-project.vercel.app'
  };
</script>
<script src="https://eabt-ai-team-project.vercel.app/widget.js"></script>

10.3 Placement

Paste the embed code just before the closing </body> tag of the webpage. It works on any HTML page regardless of the platform (WordPress, Webflow, Wix, plain HTML).

10.4 Customising the Theme Color

Add the theme property to match your school's brand color:

theme: { primaryColor: '#006633' }  // Babcock green example

11. Troubleshooting

IssueCauseResolution
Widget not appearing on website Config missing or incorrect schoolId Check that window.SchoolBotConfig is set before the <script src> tag. Verify schoolId is 'backock' or 'abu'.
Bot gives wrong or outdated answers Knowledge base is stale Upload updated documents in the Knowledge Base section. Delete the old ones.
Invite link expired Links expire after 24 hours Super Admin removes the user and sends a fresh invite.
Admin cannot see Team Members page User is Admin role, not Super Admin A Super Admin must upgrade the user's role to Super Admin if required.
Visitor messages not appearing in Escalations Polling delay The panel refreshes every 4 seconds. Wait a moment or reload the page.
Admin reply not delivered to visitor Visitor closed the widget Replies are delivered live. If the visitor reopens the widget, they will see all messages.
Cannot log in after password reset Browser caching issue Clear browser cache and cookies, then try again. Use a private/incognito window to test.

12. Security & Compliance

12.1 Password Policy

  • Minimum 8 characters
  • Use a unique password not shared with other services
  • Change password if you suspect a breach

12.2 Access Control

  • Do not share your login credentials with anyone
  • Always sign out after each session, especially on shared devices
  • Remove team members immediately upon resignation or role change
  • Conduct a quarterly review of team members and their roles

12.3 Data Handling

  • Student contact data (names, emails, phones) collected via the widget is stored securely in Supabase
  • Do not export or share lead data outside authorised channels
  • Conversation histories are stored for support and quality purposes only

12.4 Incident Response

If you suspect a security incident (unauthorised access, data breach, suspicious admin activity):

  1. Immediately notify the Super Admin and system administrator
  2. Super Admin should remove any suspected compromised accounts
  3. Contact Edutech AI Team for system-level investigation
