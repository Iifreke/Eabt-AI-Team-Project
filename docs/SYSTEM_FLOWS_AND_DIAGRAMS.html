01

Channel Ingestion & Multi-Tenant Routing Dual Pipeline

Prospective students arrive via the Web Chat widget on the university websites or via direct WhatsApp text messages. The system detects the target institution (Babcock BU-CODEL or ABU DLC) from the phone number or widget configuration, setting up isolated conversation state and university branding.

02

Closed-Domain Knowledge Vector Search Zero Hallucination

Every student question is embedded into a 1536-dimensional vector and matched against official brochures stored in Supabase pgvector. Only officially verified chunks (admission criteria, tuition schedules, semester breakdowns) are fed into the LLM context.

03

CRM Synchronization & Escalation Routing Automated Sync

Student leads are scored (HOT/WARM/COLD) and immediately synchronized to Zoho CRM. If a student requests a human advisor during business hours (Mon–Fri 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM WAT), a real-time Zoho Cliq alert fires with a direct 1-click link for instant staff takeover.

Meta Graph API v21.0
A

Intelligent Onboarding & Profile Verification

When an unverified student contacts the university WhatsApp line, the system greets them courteously and captures their full name and email address. If they are an existing applicant in the system, it displays a confirmation prompt with their saved profile, allowing them to proceed with 1 click.

B

Interactive Native Buttons with Universal Text Fallback

Students receive Meta Cloud native interactive buttons (Explore Courses, Tuition & Fees, How to Apply). If the student is using an older device or WhatsApp Web version that rejects buttons, the engine automatically falls back to an elegant numbered menu (Reply 1, 2, or 3) so no applicant is ever blocked.

C

Formatted WhatsApp Typography

All AI outputs are automatically parsed through a dedicated Markdown converter that transforms HTML/standard Markdown into native WhatsApp formatting (*bold*, _italics_, clean emoji bullets), giving admissions messages a tailored, professional appearance.

01

Intelligent Escalation Detection & Business Hours Logic

The platform instantly recognizes human handoff triggers (e.g. "speak to an agent", "talk to human", or deep complaints). It evaluates West Africa Time (WAT) against official admissions hours (Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM WAT).

02

Instant Zoho Cliq Alert Card with Direct Deep-Link

Staff members receive a mobile and desktop notification on their dedicated university Cliq channel (#babcock-admissions or #abu-admissions). The card contains the student's name, phone, program of interest, and a one-click button: [Open Live Chat in Admin Portal] that navigates directly to that conversation.

03

One-Click Takeover & Bi-Directional Delivery

Clicking Take Over pauses the AI assistant. When the admissions officer types a reply, it is routed straight to the student's phone via the Meta WhatsApp Cloud API or directly to their web browser. When the chat is resolved, internal staff notes and the full transcript are attached to the Zoho CRM Lead.

Offline Ticket Management & Executive Email Engine

Handling after-hours inquiries with automated confirmation and executive email replies.
✉️ Support Ticket & Direct Email Dispatch Cycle
Resend API + SMTP Gmail Fallback
01

Clean Offline Submission & Automatic Confirmation

Outside working hours, prospective students are presented with a streamlined support ticket modal. Upon submission, the student immediately receives an official acknowledgement receipt confirming their ticket tracking number.

02

One-Click Official Email Composer in Admin Desk

Admissions staff do not need to open an external email client. They open the ticket on the Tickets tab, review the inquiry, type their reply, and click ✉️ Send Email Reply. The system generates an executive HTML email bearing university coats of arms and official admissions signatures.

03

Dual Engine Redundancy (Resend + SMTP Failover)

Email delivery is orchestrated primarily via Resend's high-deliverability API. If any API threshold or network failure occurs, the engine instantly and transparently falls back to Gmail SMTP delivery, ensuring zero dropped messages.

Diagram Flow 05

Zoho CRM Synchronization & Lead Intelligence

Automated lead qualification, phone deduplication, and full transcript preservation.
🏢 Zoho CRM Entity Relationship & Sync Architecture
REST API v2 OAuth 2.0
01

Phone Deduplication & Lead Reconciliation

Before creating a record, the integration normalizes phone numbers into E.164 and local Nigerian formats. It queries Zoho CRM to prevent duplicates, merging multiple touchpoints (Web Chat + WhatsApp) into a single customer profile.

02

Automated Transcript Attachment as CRM Notes

Every conversation transcript—including both student inquiries and admissions advisor replies—is formatted with timestamps and saved directly as an official Note under the student's Zoho Lead profile, providing admissions officers with complete history.

03

High-Priority Escalation Task Creation

Whenever an urgent escalation occurs, the system automatically creates a High-Priority Task due the same day in Zoho CRM, ensuring admissions managers can track unresolved inquiries against SLAs.
