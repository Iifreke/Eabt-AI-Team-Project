✅ What Was Built
💬

Live Chat (Chats Page)

Agents see pending chats, expand them to read history, and reply in real time. Refreshes every 4 seconds.

🎫

Tickets

Visitors can leave offline messages from the widget. Team manages them in the Tickets page — assign, reply, close.

⚡

Canned Shortcuts

Pre-write common replies. Type / in the chat box to pick a shortcut instantly.

🌟

CSAT Rating

Visitors rate their experience (😞–😊) after a live chat. Scores appear in the Overview.

🔔

Desktop Notifications

Browser alert + sound when a new chat request arrives — no need to keep the tab in focus.

👁️

Monitoring

Live view of all active AI conversations. See who is chatting right now, updated every 30 seconds.

📊

Enhanced Overview

Stats by Today / Week / Month: total contacts, served chats, AI vs human resolved, avg CSAT, open tickets.

🟢

Agent Status

Set yourself Online / Away / Invisible from the sidebar. Status saved to the database.

🧪 How to Test — Step by Step

You need two browser windows open at the same time: one as the visitor (widget), one as the agent (admin).

Window 1 — Visitor (Widget)
1

Open the test widget page

Go to edutechbabcockabu.vercel.app/test-widget.html and click the blue chat bubble.

2

Complete onboarding

Enter your name, email, and phone when prompted by Maverick (the AI).

3

Request a human agent

Type I want to speak to a human or ask a question the bot cannot answer 3 times. The bot will escalate and notify admins.

Window 2 — Agent (Admin)
4

Log in to the admin dashboard

Go to edutechbabcockabu.vercel.app and sign in with your agent account.

5

Watch for the notification

A red badge appears on Chats in the sidebar. If browser notifications are allowed, a desktop popup fires too.

6

Open the Chats page and expand the row

Click the visitor's row. You will see their details, conversation history, and the Live Chat panel below.

7

Click "Serve" then reply

Click Serve to take the chat. Type a reply and press Enter.
In Window 1 (widget), the visitor should see your message within a few seconds.

8

Test shortcuts

Type / in the reply box. If you have saved any shortcuts (Shortcuts page), they appear as a dropdown.

9

End the chat and check ratings

Click End Chat. In the widget, the visitor can click Rate this chat ★ and submit a score. Check Overview to see the CSAT update.

10

Test offline ticket

In the widget, click ✉ Leave a message, fill in Subject and message, and submit. Then check the Tickets page in the admin — the ticket should appear there.

💡 Tip: Use an incognito window for the visitor side so it doesn't conflict with your admin session.
